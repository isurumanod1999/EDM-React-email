Direction B

Quiet Enterprise

This direction assumes the screen will be shown to clients as often as it is used by operators, so it lowers its voice: warm paper surfaces instead of flat white, one soft shadow level, and roomier fields that survive a projector. Command load is managed by hierarchy rather than grouping labels — Save, Export and Import stay on the bar while the long tail moves into a single More overflow. Type runs a step larger and dividers a step lighter, trading raw density for legibility and calm.
